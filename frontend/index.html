About this project

ChestView classifies the view position of a chest X-ray. It does not diagnose any disease.

PA view

Posterior-Anterior. The X-ray beam enters from the back and exits through the front of the chest. Usually taken with the patient standing.

AP view

Anterior-Posterior. The beam enters from the front and exits through the back. Common for patients who are bedridden or cannot stand.
